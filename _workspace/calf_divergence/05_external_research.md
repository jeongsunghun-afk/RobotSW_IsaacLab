## 2. Synthesis (≤300 words)

### Most-cited known causes for "single joint saturates early in training"
1. **Action pipeline too wide vs. PD's reach.** IsaacLab Discussion [#4121](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/discussions/4121) explicitly documents that `action_scale × actor_output` can exceed what realistic stiffness/damping can track, so the joint target rails and the policy never sees the joint converge — its only feedback is "more rail."
2. **PPO log_std degeneracy / entropy collapse.** rsl_rl issue [#33](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/rsl_rl/issues/33) shows actor `std` can go NaN; literature ([2512.06486](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06486), [2509.26114](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.26114) on clip-asymmetric entropy bias, [2405.00662](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.00662)) shows premature determinism and representation collapse are common quadruped failure modes.
3. **Reward shape rewarding the saturated mode.** Survey [2410.10438](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10438) and the extreme-parkour pattern (`clip(rew_buf, min=0)` per issue [#59](https://github.com/chengxuxin/extreme-parkour/issues/59)) make degenerate gaits (extended-leg base_height, abnormal feet_air_time) a known attractor.

### Most-cited fixes
- **Tighten / grow** the effective action range early ([GPO](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20668v1)).
- **Raise PD stiffness/damping** to realistic envelope and **scale actions** so commands are feasible (IsaacLab #4121).
- **Adaptive entropy** instead of fixed `entropy_coef` ([2512.06486](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.06486)).
- **Action priors / default-pose regularization** ([APEX](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.10022)).
- **Instrument** actor `std`, KL, and per-joint action histograms to catch collapse early (rsl_rl #33).

### Closest match to user's symptom
**IsaacLab Discussion [#4121](https://github.com/isaac-sim/IsaacLab/discussions/4121)** is the closest publicly documented analog: "policy actions are too high and don't match actual joint pos." Combined with the upstream confirmation that `clip(rew_buf, min=0)` is intentional (extreme-parkour [#59](https://github.com/chengxuxin/extreme-parkour/issues/59)), the external evidence points first at the **action→PD→joint feasibility loop** and second at **per-joint exploration noise / entropy schedule**, not at the reward clip.
